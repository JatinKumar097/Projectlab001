Emergency help is your choice

Share location or call local emergency services when you choose. No automatic police dispatch.

Nightline is a front-end prototype, not an emergency service. If you’re in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

Safety includes privacy

Your face and location stay yours.

Face recognition is not enabled in this prototype. A production version should use a properly secured, opt-in identity provider and explain exactly what biometric data is processed, where it is stored, and how to delete it. A check-in should never depend on facial recognition.

This page does not request camera or location access, store trip data, send SMS, or contact family or emergency services. The interactive check-in is a local demonstration only.

When a real alert is needed

Any location sharing should be a deliberate choice, visible to you, and sent only to a contact you selected or an emergency service you explicitly request. Missed check-ins alone should never trigger an automatic police dispatch.
